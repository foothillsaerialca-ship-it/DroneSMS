# DroneSMS Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026  
**Version:** `2026-10-30-v1`

This Privacy Policy describes how DroneSMS handles information when you use the DroneSMS application and related support services. DroneSMS is an operational safety-management tool for commercial drone operators. This policy is a product disclosure, not legal advice; your organization remains responsible for its own privacy notices and legal obligations.

## Information we process

- **Account details:** sign-in email, authentication identifiers, names, and account-security state.
- **Organization details:** company contact information, branding, settings, and membership relationships.
- **Operational records:** proposals, job schedules and locations, personnel and crew assignments, aircraft and equipment records, JHAs, airspace and weather evidence, pre-flight checks, safety events, closeouts, acknowledgements, corrective-action records, and management-of-change records.
- **Uploaded files:** evidence selected by users, such as photographs, equipment references, and organization logos.
- **Technical and security information:** authentication/session data, service logs, request metadata, and abuse-prevention signals needed to operate and secure the service.

## How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, provide organization-scoped workflows, preserve safety and audit records, generate requested documents, secure and troubleshoot the service, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. DroneSMS does not provide flight-control or airspace-authorization services.

## Sharing and service providers

Information is available to authorized members of the organization that records or receives it, subject to access controls. DroneSMS relies on Supabase for authentication, database, and storage services and Cloudflare for application hosting and delivery. Providers process information to supply their services under their own contractual and security terms. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

## Storage, security, and retention

Data is stored using the configured service providers and access controls, including organization-scoped database policies. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Operational and safety records may be retained while an organization account is active and as needed for service integrity, legal obligations, dispute resolution, backups, and security. Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove copies in protected backups or records that must be retained.

## Your choices and requests

Users should contact their organization administrator for access, correction, or deletion of organization-managed records. For account, privacy, or support requests, contact [support@dronesms.app](mailto:support@dronesms.app). Requests are reviewed and handled as required by applicable law and operational retention needs.

## Children and changes

DroneSMS is intended for business use and is not directed to children. We may update this policy as the service changes. When a material update requires renewed acceptance, the service records a new policy version and asks users to review it before continuing.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to [support@dronesms.app](mailto:support@dronesms.app).
