Framework

Built Around the ICAO SMS Standard.

Every feature, template, and workflow in DroneSMS maps to one of the four pillars of the ICAO Safety Management System framework, used across commercial aviation worldwide.

01

Safety Policy

Operator commitments, crew roles, authority structure, emergency contacts, and stop-work authority.

02

Risk Management

Site intake, JHA, airspace review, LAANC, hazard identification, and pre-job risk rating.

03

Safety Assurance

Preflight checklists, incident reporting, deviation logging, flight records, and equipment inspection.

04

Safety Promotion

Employee training logs, crew briefing templates, and client-facing safety communication.
